# Cookie Policy

**Last updated:** 10 August 2026

## What we use

Servio **does not set profiling or tracking cookies**. We use **localStorage** and **sessionStorage** only for technically necessary service functionality.

## Storage used

| Key | Type | Purpose | Duration |
|-----|------|---------|----------|
| `bo_session` | localStorage | Guest session ID for orders | Persistent until cleared |
| `bo_access_*` | sessionStorage | QR access token (anti-abuse) | Browser session |
| `bo_cart:*` | localStorage | Cart contents | Persistent |
| `bo_active_order:*` | localStorage | Active order reference | Persistent |
| `bo_token` | localStorage | Staff auth token | Until logout |
| `bo_tenant_slug` | localStorage | Active staff tenant | Until logout |
| `bo_locale` | localStorage | Language preference | Persistent |
| `bo_cookie_consent` | localStorage | Cookie consent choice | Persistent |

## Consent

For guests accessing the menu via QR, we show an informational banner on first visit. Consent covers the use of local storage required for the app to work (cart, order session). If declined, some features (e.g. persistent cart) may be unavailable.

## Third-party cookies

We currently do not use third-party cookies. If introduced in the future (e.g. analytics), this policy will be updated and explicit consent will be requested.

## Managing preferences

You can clear localStorage data from your browser settings. To erase guest session data associated with your device, use "Delete my data" in the customer menu (where available) or contact the data controller (the beach establishment).
